# Instrument sample credits

These four banks contain a subset of the MP3 note renders from **FluidR3_GM**, the FluidR3 sound bank by **Frank Wen**, packaged by **gleitz / MIDI.js Soundfonts**.

Harmonica, clean electric guitar, orchestral harp and violin each retain 13 chromatic MP3 notes from the source bank. The base64 audio is unchanged; the packaging and selected note range were changed for DJ EARth. Playback can transpose samples to the requested pitch and apply gain and a release fade. These are sample-based SoundFont renders, not newly recorded performances. No endorsement by the original creators is implied.

Exact upstream URLs and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in `sources.json`. Harmonica, harp and violin cover C4–C5; electric guitar covers C3–C4. The app loads the banks locally on demand, with no third-party audio request during playback.

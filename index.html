Brightness → sound

One brightness value controls two sound parameters: pitch and amplitude.

Sound off. Start sound, then move the slider.

For this test: brightness 0–255 maps to pitch 220–880 Hz and amplitude 0–100%. Brighter means higher and louder; zero brightness is silent.

This is a chosen mapping to sound, not a measurement of light frequency.
